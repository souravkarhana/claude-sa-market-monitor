Ye bina stop-loss ke 5 din hold ka result hai. Avg return chhota hai aur edge asli me sirf Oversold me clear hai. Data sirf aaj chal rahe stocks ka hai (delisted nahi), isliye result thoda zyada acha dikh sakta hai.

MR20 = % stocks 20 EMA ke upar. U4/D4 = 4%+ chadhe/gire (volume badhne ke saath). W20 = 5 din me 20%+ chadhe stocks. Ye market breadth ka tool hai, trading salah nahi. Naya din roz shaam ko bhavcopy aane ke baad judta hai.
